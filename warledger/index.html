War Ledger.

Reads the daily points ranking out of clan-war screenshots, lines the numbers up with your roster, and hands you back one column you can paste straight into Data Entry. Everything runs in your browser — no upload, no key, no server.

STEP 1

Paste the roster

The names in the order your spreadsheet uses. Pull them from the sheet, or paste and edit by hand — whatever's in the box is what gets used, and it's remembered in this browser. Blank lines are kept, so the column you get out lines up row for row.

Where the names come from

Reads that column from that row downwards and takes it as it comes. The sheet has to be viewable by anyone with the link — nothing here signs you in. The link stays in this browser and is never part of the page, so it doesn't end up in the repo.

STEP 2

Add the screenshots

Full-screen shots of the ranking popup. Overlap between shots is fine — duplicates get merged. You can also just paste with Ctrl+V.
